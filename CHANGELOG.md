# Changelog

## 0.7
- Big water bottles: see-through blue plastic with ribs and a blue cap, glossy in the sun.
  They stand in towers, crown the seesaw's loads, sit under arches and on the summits.
- A new kind of building: a rack of shelves lined with bottles.
- A bottle bursts at a hard blow or a hard landing: a wet slap, a fan of glittering spray,
  a puff of mist. A burst bottle counts as knocked off.
- Water stays: a puddle spreads where it burst; on the plinth it pours over the front
  edge, runs down the stone in streaks and pools at the foot. Puddles dry in half a minute.

## 0.6
- People are round now: shaded heads, tapered limbs, shorts, trousers or skirts, long
  hair, hats and bags.
- Cars are little sedans with windscreens, side glass, chrome bumpers, lights and plates;
  a row of them is parked along both curbs, and traffic cones stand by the plinth.
- Shadows of people and cars are soft, with blurred edges and fading tails.
- A warm fill light on everything that faces the thrower; a stronger glow on the sun side
  of the sky.

## 0.5
- Evening by the sea. A low sun from the left lays long, dark shadows across the road:
  dappled shadows of palm crowns, the shadow of the plinth and of every piece.
- To the left a promenade with two rows of palms, a stone parapet and the sea glittering
  beyond it; to the right a row of old pastel houses with shutters, iron balconies,
  flower pots and striped awnings over the shops. Lamp posts, bollards, cafe parasols.
- An evening sky, deep blue above and warm by the sun, with streaks of pink cloud.
- Buildings are half as big again, on a bigger granite plinth; the hammer is heavier.
- People and cars in colour, lit by the low sun, each with a long shadow.

## 0.4
- The plinth stands farther off, and the hammer flies a real arc: slower, higher, with a
  pale wake behind its head. The hammer is heavier to make up for it.
- The street keeps to the background: people walk by without noticing the game, cars
  come, turn round and drive back. They are drawn as soft silhouettes with long
  shadows, and make no sound.

## 0.3
- The street is alive: people stroll along both pavements, some in straw hats or dark
  glasses. They stop to watch a throw, jump at a crash and run off.
- Cars come down the road, stop to look and turn round. If what they saw was a ruin,
  they honk, turn with screaming tyres and floor it in a cloud of exhaust.

## 0.2
- The street is seen at a slant: the thrower stands in the left lane.
- The plinth stands farther down the street.

## 0.1
- First playable: the street, the plinth, seven kinds of buildings, the hammer that
  comes back, five stars, the instant retry, synthesized sound.
